Current State

This tenant has a meaningful governance gap in one area and a critical unmitigated risk in another. 26 of its 104 Microsoft 365 groups have no owner — and every one of the tenant's 18 Teams has no designated owner. No group expiration policy exists, and no access reviews have been configured for the 24 guest accounts currently in the directory. Guest invitation permissions are set to the most permissive available option: any licensed user can invite an external guest with no restriction. Cross-tenant access policy is configured, which provides some structural boundary, but the lack of periodic review means guests accumulate without audit.

On the positive side, all 99 SharePoint sites carry a sensitivity label, all 18 Teams have both a meeting policy and a messaging policy assigned, and no guest accounts show signs of staleness by the telemetry available. No access reviews are overdue because none were ever created. The Microsoft 365 Message Center holds 440 items, 108 flagged as major changes — these require a triage pass to identify which require tenant-side action before their deadlines.

Domain 01
Ownership & Accountability
NO-GO 25% of groups have no owner 26 / 104 groups

26 of the tenant's 104 Microsoft 365 groups have no owner. An ownerless group has no accountable person to approve membership changes, respond to access requests, or confirm whether the group is still needed. These groups include production-looking names such as Finance, Payroll, Billing, Legal, Sales, Help Desk, and Human Solutions, alongside several client-named groups. Without an owner, membership in these groups is effectively unmanaged.

The consequence for security and compliance is direct: any content stored in or shared through an ownerless group cannot be attested to by a responsible individual, which undermines any access review or audit process.

NO-GO Every Team in the tenant has no designated owner 18 / 18 Teams

All 18 Teams — including ATRIO, Small Parts Manufacturing, Roofing CRM, Netropole, TARR, Terminal Transfer, TCMS, Template, LifeWorksNW, Hunter Davisson, Vigilant, Sales, Columbia River Carbonates, Barricade MSP, NASA, Test NASA, Finance, and Shane McCaw Consulting - Templates — have no owner recorded. No owner means no one is responsible for approving guest additions to those Teams, managing channels, or deciding when the Team should be archived. This is particularly significant because all 18 of these Teams currently have guest members.

WARNING All group memberships are maintained by hand 0 / 104 dynamic

None of the tenant's 104 groups use dynamic membership rules. Every group is maintained manually. Manual membership management is operationally viable at small scale, but at 104 groups it creates a sustained administrative burden and increases the risk that stale membership persists after employees change roles or leave the organisation. When group owners are also absent — as they are for 26 of these groups — there is no one checking membership at all.

Domain 02
Lifecycle — Creation, Review & Retirement
NO-GO No group expiration policy is configured

The tenant has no Microsoft 365 group expiration policy. Without one, groups and Teams created today will exist indefinitely unless an administrator manually deletes them. The tenant already has 104 groups, 26 of which have no owner and would have no one to respond to an expiry notification even if a policy were applied. Expiration policy is the primary automated mechanism for forcing periodic attestation: groups are either renewed by their owner or removed. Its absence means the current 104-group estate will grow unchecked.

NO-GO No access reviews exist — for any resource

Zero access reviews are configured in this tenant. The telemetry shows no overdue reviews, but only because there are no reviews of any kind to become overdue. There is no periodic attestation process for group membership, guest access, or any other entitlement. This means no one is routinely confirming who should still have access to what. Combined with 18 Teams that each have guest members and no owner, this creates a sustained, unaudited exposure.

PASS No inactive SharePoint sites identified 0 / 99 sites inactive

The scan evaluated 99 SharePoint sites and identified none as inactive. Last-modification timestamps were not available in this scan pass, so the inactive count reflects what the platform returned. No action is required from this finding.

Domain 03
Sharing & External Access
NO-GO Guest invitations are open to everyone — no restriction configured

The tenant's B2B collaboration setting is configured to allow anyone — any licensed user in the tenant — to invite an external guest with no restriction. This is the broadest available setting. There is no requirement for admin approval, no restriction to specific domains, and no requirement that the inviter hold a particular role. Combined with the fact that all 18 Teams have no owner, this means guests can be added to Teams workspaces by any user, with no owner to review or remove them afterward.

NO-GO No guest access reviews configured — 24 guests in the directory 24 guests · 0 reviews

There are 24 guest accounts in the tenant directory and zero guest access reviews configured. The telemetry could not confirm the last sign-in date for any of the 24 guests, which means their activity status is unknown. No guest can currently be confirmed as still needing access — and no automated process will flag or remove them when they no longer do. This is the compounding failure: the invitation setting lets guests in freely, and the absence of reviews means they never have to justify continued access.

PASS Cross-tenant access policy is active

A cross-tenant access policy is configured on this tenant. This provides a structural control point for inbound and outbound collaboration with other Microsoft 365 tenants, and is a positive governance baseline. Its presence does not offset the absence of guest access reviews or the unrestricted invitation setting, but it confirms that the tenant boundary has been deliberately addressed.

WARNING Every Team has at least one guest member — and no owner 18 / 18 Teams

All 18 Teams have guests as members. That fact alone is not a governance failure — guest collaboration is a legitimate use of Teams. The failure is that each of those 18 Teams also has no owner. There is no person accountable for managing or reviewing the guest memberships in any of them. If a guest relationship ends — a contract closes, a client relationship changes — the guest's access to that Team's channels and files persists until someone notices and removes it manually.

Domain 04
Structure, Labelling & Policy Enforcement
PASS All SharePoint sites carry a sensitivity label 99 / 99 sites labelled

Every one of the 99 SharePoint sites evaluated has a sensitivity label applied. Full label coverage is a significant positive: it means classification has been applied at the site level and provides the foundation for downstream controls such as conditional access, external sharing restrictions, and data loss prevention. This is the strongest governance result in the tenant.

PASS All 18 Teams have both a meeting policy and a messaging policy assigned

All 18 Teams have a meeting policy assigned and all 18 have a messaging policy assigned. Policy coverage is complete. The policies that govern how meetings are conducted and how users can message within those Teams are in place across the entire Teams estate.

WARNING Test and unnamed channels present in the Teams estate 27 channels evaluated

The scan identified 27 channels across the Teams estate. Among them are channels named "Test", "Test 123", "Test 12345", and "Test 12345" — alongside purpose-built channels such as Intranet - HR Compliance, Lookup System, CFS System, SharePoint Advanced Search, SharePoint Upgrade, and Storage. The presence of multiple test-named channels in a production estate indicates that provisioning is not consistently governed: channels are created ad hoc and not removed when testing is complete. One Team is publicly visible — Test NASA — which means its content is discoverable to all internal users without requiring a join request.

WARNING One Team is configured as Public 1 / 18 Teams public

The Team named Test NASA is configured as Public. A public Team is visible and joinable by any user inside the tenant without approval. Its name suggests it was created for testing and was never intended as a permanent workspace. If the Team contains any content — even placeholder or test material — that content is accessible to the entire tenant. The Team should be confirmed as intentional, converted to Private, or removed.

Domain 05
Platform Change Awareness
WARNING 108 major platform change announcements require review 108 major · 440 total

The Microsoft 365 Message Center holds 440 items. Of those, 108 are categorised as major changes — announcements from Microsoft that a feature is changing in a way that may require administrator action or affect users noticeably. An additional set are categorised as "Plan for change" items, meaning action is expected before a deadline. A further subset are categorised as "Prevent or fix issue," meaning something is actively wrong or will break without intervention.

The scan cannot determine which of these 108 major-change items have been reviewed or acted on — that determination requires a human triage pass. Items confirmed as relevant in this estate include the Exchange Web Services retirement (affecting any integration using EWS), the SharePoint Alerts retirement, the SharePoint Designer 2013 end of support on 14 July 2026, the retirement of legacy Microsoft Whiteboard data (requiring migration to OneDrive-backed whiteboards), the legacy TLS retirement for POP and IMAP connections, and the requirement to update Microsoft 365 Apps above version 16.0.18827.20202. Each of these has a hard deadline and a consequence for tenants that do not act.

Required Actions
What to Fix, Ordered by Impact

Each action below is derived from a confirmed finding above. Actions are ordered by the scale of the governance gap they close, not by implementation effort.

# Action Severity What It Closes
1 Assign an owner to every ownerless group. Start with the 26 groups that currently have none, prioritising those with sensitive names: Finance, Payroll, Billing, Legal, Sales, Help Desk, Human Solutions. For groups that cannot be attributed to a live employee, decide whether to assign an admin as a placeholder owner or archive and delete the group. CRITICAL Ownerless groups — 26 of 104
2 Assign an owner to each of the 18 Teams. The ownerless-Teams state is total: every Team in the estate has this problem. Assign at minimum one owner per Team from the person most responsible for that workspace's work. This is a prerequisite for any guest review process. CRITICAL All 18 Teams unowned
3 Configure guest access reviews in Microsoft Entra ID Governance. Set a recurring review — quarterly is a practical starting cadence — scoped to the 24 guest accounts currently in the directory. Route each review to the Team or group owner once those are assigned. Without this, guest accounts persist indefinitely. CRITICAL 24 guests, zero reviews
4 Restrict who can invite guests. Change the guest invitation setting from "everyone" to at minimum "members and admins" — or, if the organisation requires it, to admins only. The current setting allows any licensed user to invite any external person with no approval required. This does not need to wait for Teams ownership to be resolved. CRITICAL Unrestricted guest invitations
5 Enable a Microsoft 365 group expiration policy. Set a renewal period — 180 days is a common starting point for active environments. Configure it to notify the group owner when expiry approaches. For the 26 groups that have no owner, the notification will go unactioned; that is why action 1 must precede or run concurrently with this. HIGH No lifecycle control on 104 groups
6 Triage the 108 major-change Message Center items. Prioritise any item categorised as "Prevent or fix issue" and any "Plan for change" item with a 2025 or mid-2026 deadline. Specific items requiring near-term decisions include the Exchange Web Services retirement, SharePoint Alerts retirement, SharePoint Designer 2013 end of support (14 July 2026), legacy Microsoft Whiteboard migration, and the legacy TLS retirement for POP and IMAP. HIGH 108 unreviewed major changes
7 Review and remove or rename test channels and the public Team. Confirm whether the channels named "Test", "Test 123", and "Test 12345" serve any ongoing purpose and remove them if not. Convert the Team named Test NASA to Private or delete it — a public Team with a test name has no place in a governed production estate. MODERATE Channel sprawl · public Team exposure
8 Evaluate whether dynamic group membership is appropriate for any recurring access patterns. All 104 groups are currently maintained by hand. For groups that represent a role, department, or consistent attribute — such as Finance, Sales, or Help Desk — dynamic membership rules would automate membership accuracy and reduce the risk of stale access. This is a medium-term improvement, not an immediate remediation. MODERATE Manual membership across 104 groups
