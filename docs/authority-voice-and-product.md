## The real product, today

**M365 Copilot Readiness Assessment.** A paid, consent-gated, real-data assessment of a
Microsoft 365 tenant's readiness for Copilot — scored across 6 pillars (Governance, Security,
Compliance, Licensing, Adoption, Health), delivered through a live scan and a set of
tenant-specific documents.

**How it works:** a prospect answers a short quiz, consents to a real scan of their M365 tenant
via Microsoft Graph and PowerShell execution, and the platform runs real checks — never
self-reported, never a generic checklist. Results are revealed through a scan experience that
never fabricates a score from missing data: genuinely clean, never evaluated, and found problems
are always shown as three distinct, honest states.

**Differentiators, real and specific:**
- Real Graph API telemetry, not a self-report checklist.
- Honest three-state verdict logic — never fakes a passing score from zero real signal.
- Document generation grounded in the platform's own signal engines, not generic AI text.
- Per-pillar grading, never one lump score — a real tenant is multi-pillar, and a customer is
  never judged on controls they were never assessed against.

**Who it's for:** IT/security leadership at mid-market and regulated commercial organizations
evaluating or already licensed for Microsoft 365 Copilot. Gov/GCC/GCC-High tenants are
explicitly out of scope.
