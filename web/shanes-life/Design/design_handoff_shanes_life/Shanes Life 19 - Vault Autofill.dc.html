19Shane's Life · Vault Autofill · the Chrome add-on · all groups

One Face ID a month, not one per password.

The add-on in extension/ treats every fill as a reveal, so every login costs a Face ID. That is the part Shane won't live with. This sheet keeps the vault's crypto and audit trail exactly as built and adds the one thing LastPass has: a trusted device. Face ID once on this Chrome, then thirty days of one-click fills. Banks you mark Always ask still ask.

What a fill costs

  • Trusted Chrome (default 30 days): one click on the chip. No prompt.
  • Always-ask entry (banks, the mortgage servicer): Face ID every time, even when trusted.
  • First time, or trust ended: Face ID once in the app's own popup window, with "Trust this Chrome for 30 days" on by default.
  • Sign out everywhere in Settings, or Forget this device, ends the trust instantly.

What changes in the repo

  • vault_device_trust: same shape as widget_tokens (high-entropy value handed out once, SHA-256 stored, label, expires_at = 30 days, revoked_at), minted only at the end of a real WebAuthn reveal ceremony.
  • POST /api/vault/:id/fill with the trust token: returns the plaintext once, writes a vault_reveals row with via = 'extension-trusted'. Refuses always-ask entries (they take the existing reveal path).
  • vault.always_ask boolean default false; set from the Vault room ("Navy Federal always asks") or the options page.
  • Trusted devices list in Settings → Connected and in the Vault room's add-on card; "Sign out everywhere" revokes them all.
