Balloons, with the sky they float in

Same idea as 1b, more alive. The section is a patch of sky, not a list with balloon icons: a soft blue wash, clouds at three depths drifting at three speeds, and a bird that flaps across every twenty seconds.

The balloons

  • Bigger, patchwork envelopes in the moment's color with a light center gore, a scalloped band and a shine; a little red pennant on top.
  • A woven basket with a rim and two sandbags. The critter rides in it, head over the rim.
  • The note is a luggage tag tied to the basket by a real string, through a punched hole in the card. Tag and balloon float together, the tag half a beat behind, so the pair feels tethered.

Still true

  • Balloons alternate sides; right-hand ones face inward. One tap per moment. Heading out unfolds under its tag.
  • Five, two or none: the sky is the same size either way, and empty means the koala napping on his cloud while the bird passes.
  • Tap a balloon and it lifts off the top of the screen into its moment; that's the transition. The Rooms house waits below, so the sky sits where a sky should.

1Shane's Life · Later, by moment · a scene, not a list · shown at five, two and zero

Later, as a place

Later, by moment is the one part of Today that still reads as a list. Moments come and go: some days five, some days none, so whatever holds them has to grow and shrink and still look finished when it's empty. Two ways to do that, each shown with five moments, two, and nothing.

1a · The signpost

  • A crossroads sign at the fork in the paw trail. Every moment is a board pointing somewhere your day might go, and that moment's critter rides on its board. Paw prints lead off each arrow.
  • A bird keeps watch on top and glances around. The lantern is lit after dark.
  • Boards alternate sides and sit a degree off level, like someone nailed them up by hand. With nothing later, one small board says so.

1b · The balloons

  • Moments float. Each is a hot-air balloon in that moment's color, with its critter in the basket and its note tied alongside. Balloons drift a little, each on its own rhythm; clouds pass behind.
  • With nothing later, the koala naps on his cloud in an empty sky.

Both

  • One tap per moment. Heading out still unfolds its packing list in place. Nothing scrolls sideways; five fit above Meds.
  • Motion is drift, glance and flicker. Nothing bounces.
