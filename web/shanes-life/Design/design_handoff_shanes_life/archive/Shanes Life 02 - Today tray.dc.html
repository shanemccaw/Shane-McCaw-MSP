4Shane's Life · Today · the house, furnished and alive

The house, furnished

Same house as 3a, now lived in. Lamp on means something in that room needs doing. Lamp off means nothing does, and the critter is asleep.

Every room has its things

  • Shopping: striped awning, two stocked shelves, a sign swinging on its string.
  • Recipes: pot rack, stove, a pot steaming, basil on the sill.
  • Dates: a calendar with tomorrow circled, a clock whose second hand sweeps, dawn in the window.
  • Pets: a bed with a bone, the leash on its hook, a ball, a fish going back and forth.
  • People: three photos on the wall, a plant that sways.
  • Money: a safe, a coin stack, the first dollar framed, a coin dropping into the piggy bank.
  • Things: a round attic window, boxes, a paint can, dust in the light.
  • Lists: a pencil cup, a pinboard, one note fluttering.

Motion rules

  • Everything drifts, sways or sweeps. Nothing bounces or pops.
  • Loops run 3 to 20 seconds, so the house feels alive without being busy.
  • Lit lamps breathe. Sleepers get a small z. The snail crosses the doorstep about once every 20 seconds. Smoke from the chimney while a stove is on.
  • Same critters as everywhere else, same daily roll. The koala and snail are today's idle pair, so they nap on the roof and the doorstep.

Rollout

  • The house is one component with eight room slots; each room's furniture is a small drawing that ships with the room. Lamp state comes from the same rule that fills the one-liner.

3Shane's Life · Today, the mix · 2b with one big peeker on Next and a house for the rooms

The critters move into one house

Everything from 2b stays: the fox talks, later is a paw trail, meds is a pill, the frog holds the capture bar. Two changes: Next borrows 2a's peeker, one critter and bigger; and Rooms becomes the house the critters live in.

Next

  • One peeker. Today's cat hangs over the Next card alone, a third taller than in 2a, paws on the edge. Eyes still glance every few seconds.

Idle pair

  • The snail and the koala still roll daily, but now they hang around the house: the koala naps on the roof, the snail passes the corner.

Rooms · the house

  • A cutaway house. Eight rooms on four floors, one critter living in each, with the thing they keep: jars, a stove, a coin stack, a clock, a bowl, boxes, a photo, a pinboard.
  • Lights on means something's inside. Shopping, Recipes and Dates have their lamp lit today; the rest are dark and quiet. No badges, no red counts.
  • One line under each name says what's inside, so you rarely have to walk in to check.
  • The ground floor is the thumb floor. Shopping and Money sit nearest the capture bar; Things and Lists live in the attic.
  • Tap a room to walk in. The room grows to fill the screen, its wall color becomes the room's header, the critter walks up to the title. The little house rides along in every room as the way back, your room's lamp lit.

2Shane's Life · Today, cuter · one critter language at two dials

Cute, the way the critters already are

Both keep the contract: Next first, moments not lists, one capture bar, no charts, no scores. The cute is five moves any room can borrow. Rolled for this mock: a dawn sky, pebble-shaped blobs, houses for the rooms, and the snail and the koala as today's idle pair.

The five moves

  • Blobs, not tiles. Every critter sits in a soft pebble tinted with its own color. Nothing square holds a critter.
  • The fox talks. One sentence in a bubble at the top: what's next, or that nothing needs you. Never a nag.
  • Rounder. Cards 22, capture and primary buttons as pills, badges tilted 5° like stickers.
  • Sky. The top of Today follows the clock: peach at dawn, plain by day, violet at dusk, stars at night.
  • Quiet motion. Peeker eyes glance, the fox breathes. Nothing bounces.

What stays

  • Inter, the navy canvas, design-system buttons and badges.
  • Every roll rule from the critter sheet: same critter everywhere, all day.
  • Lucide for actions: chevrons, mic, camera.
  • No streaks, no scores, nothing red unless money is short.
