Why: the barcode identifies the item and matches it to the list; Shane only types the price. Prices are stored per store and per date, and Claude reads them over MCP (get_prices) so the next list carries real numbers instead of estimates. Saving also checks the item off. The same sheet is on the shared link, so whoever is holding the cart can scan too.

When the scan doesn't match: three cases, none of them block. Exact (barcode seen before): straight to the price. Near (new barcode, product name resembles list items): the sheet shows the product name and 2–3 candidate chips from the list plus "Something else"; one tap links the barcode to that item forever. Unknown (nothing like it on the list): "Add it to the list" or "It's one of these" (pick from the open items). Every link is remembered per barcode, so each product asks at most once. Live in the prototype: scans cycle exact → near → unknown.
