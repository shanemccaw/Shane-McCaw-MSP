17Turn 2 · FinanceHome migration: your eight screens, in Money's skin, with the real numbers

Where each FinanceHome screen lands

Same data, one model: a bill's envelope is the real NFCU balance of its account, so "assigned" and "in pocket" stop being two numbers. Available to spend keeps Safe to Spend's math (DirectDeposit + the two Spend accounts); what's still to fund sits beside it, never hidden inside it. The two-week cycle is the first card on Now and Bills: dates as the title, fourteen day cells with today marked, both paydays named, ‹ › to page back a cycle. Period Aug 28 – Sep 11, today Mon Sep 7, payday Fri Sep 11.

Mapping

  • Overview → Now. Safe to Spend keeps its math and becomes Available to spend. Cycle In / In Pockets / Still Owed collapse to one line under the pay-period bar. Attention Needed's eight alerts become four Urgent rows: a due date and its shortfall are the same bill. Distribute Paycheck shows up on payday as the Budget Day card.
  • Period Review → the cycle card at the top of Now and Bills: Aug 28 – Sep 11 as the title, day 11 of 14, fourteen cells with today in amber, Came in / Spent / Still to fund, and ‹ › paging back through earlier cycles in place of the dot carousel. Its bill list is the buckets right under it. Monthly Bills Overview is one footer line in Bills ($7,801.47 a month · $3,900.73 per check).
  • Budget → Bills. PAST DUE keeps its six rows, months and owed, with no red wall and no "19 payments missed" pill. Partially Funded chips and per-bill bars become "$1,492.40 of $2,550" plus a % badge. New Cycle happens on payday. Reorder is drag. The + is the bar.
  • Income → Accounts › Income. Sources, rules with their toggles, and Scan transactions stay. Categories become the kind label on each source; the unused six don't render.
  • Accounts → Accounts. Deposit / Emergency / Overdraft tiles fold into the gate card. Grouping by house stays (it matches Rooms). The link glyph becomes a Plaid dot.
  • Insights → gone as a tab. The bear says one thing at a time in the header; the bar answers "what if". Nudges stay rationed.
  • Flag: Paid ✓ can't be a checkbox. It's inferred when Plaid sees the debit leave the bill account. Cars and Shared account numbers weren't in the screenshots; shown collapsed with their combined balance.

Flagged, not guessed (contract §8)

  • Bills vs Accounts overlap. Both list bill accounts. Shown here as a sixth tab so nothing live moves; once the envelope model ships, Bills can fold into Accounts as its first section and the tab count drops back to five.
  • Debt tracker fields. Finance-Tracker's tracker has more than balance, plan and payoff date. Only those three are drawn; the rest needs the ported schema first.
  • Distribute paycheck = Transfer instructions when the source is the gate. They're one card here. A simulator or reassignment result reuses the same rows.
