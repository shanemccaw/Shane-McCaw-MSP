Open categories
Items carry category TEXT, not an enum. "Mom's coming the 12th–18th" becomes Visits with a date, a lead time and an icon, no deploy. A capture that fits nowhere opens a talk-it-through sheet: hold for Claude, make a category, or scope a build.
One interval engine
Appointments every 6 weeks or 2 months, rabies every 3 years, registration yearly, birthdays yearly: one interval_days plus a per-kind lead_days (1, 10, 14, 7, 30, 21). Holidays come in from OPM with no entry.
Two deliberate exceptions
Day-before appointment reminders are clock-anchored on purpose. The smoking line is the one place the app is allowed to confront: actual spend against actual shortfall, no streak, no reset. Both are Shane's calls, scoped to one feature each.

AI boundary
Tesla webhook → climate preconditioning opens Heading Out. Hits the app directly; no AI.
Sale webhook → price watcher fires the Money proposal. Rules, not a model.
Nudge budget lives in the app: 1–3 a day, whichever source they come from. Claude can push a list; it can't push a nudge.

Why: keeps §6 honest. The dashed line is the only place a model runs. The public/private split is a property of each list (a revocable token), not a second app. The nudge cap sits inside the app so nothing upstream, including Claude, can flood the tray.
