What this pack is

Every screen in the locked blueprint, drawn against the Shane McCaw system in its Deep-Navy dark mode (navy canvas, off-white text, Electric Blue actions, Inter, Lucide). Dark throughout; a light theme is one token switch away if ever wanted. Five screens get 2–3 directions to pick from; everything else is one direction. Desktop appears where a laptop is plausible (sign-in, tray, recipes, money, people, architecture); Shopping, the shared link, Meds, Heading Out and Things are phone-only by nature.

Assumptions I made

  • Dark mode is the only mode. Every screen sits on the system's dark tokens, so contrast is set once.
  • Sign-in is passkey / Face ID. No passwords anywhere. Shared links skip auth entirely.
  • Lists pushed from Claude over MCP carry a quiet From Claude tag so Shane knows where a list came from.
  • Households are named Home (hub) and Rental (spoke). Medication names are placeholders.
  • The nudge cap is shown once, quietly ("1 of up to 3 today"). No other counts that could read as a score.
  • The in-app capture box can't call Claude live (§6). Proposal: simple on-device rules route the obvious cases instantly (groceries → Aldi list); everything else waits in an Inbox that Claude tidies on its next MCP pull. See 1w.

Design moves that follow from the brief

  • The tray leads. There is no home grid; the first screen is "what's next" plus the capture bar, docked on every screen.
  • Time never appears as a trigger. Groupings are moments: Heading out, At Aldi, At the Rental, Idle.
  • Full-screen modes (Shopping, Recipes, Review) get a thin blue→teal strip under the header so they feel like a different room, not another tab.
  • Reset language instead of overdue language: "Let it go", "Not this trip", "Ask again next time at the Rental". Nothing turns red, nothing stacks.
  • Stated facts land instantly with an Undo, never a confirm. Only ambiguous captures ask, and they ask with two chips, not a form.

Reference any option by its badge (e.g. 1c) in chat. Groups run in the order Shane would meet them: sign in → tray → capture → modes → systems → sharing → architecture.

Decisions so far (Sep 6)

Recorded from chat. Turn 2 on Shopping and Recipes holds the additions.

  • Tray: 1b
  • Capture: 1e default, 1f when there's more to dump
  • Shopping: all three views, plus scan → real price 2a and aisle memory 2b
  • Recipes: 1k, plus ingredient checks 2a and the loud in-app timer 2b
  • Review: 1l
  • Meds, Money, Things, Heading Out, People, Architecture: as drawn
  • Shared list: 1t, one list with a Flat / Category / Best path toggle and Shopping mode's tools (2a), phone only, no note, no Home/Rental split
  • Tray: Review lives under Idle. Meds is its own card (two batched notifications a day: morning, before bed). Rooms are a list with icon tiles: Shopping, Recipes, Money, Things, People. The capture bar answers "where's the drill?" in place.
  • Shopping: stores are Aldi, Walmart, Publix. Prices and aisle spots are per store; items show "cheaper at Walmart $1.58" when a better recorded price exists. Coupons arrive from Claude over MCP (store, quantity, saving) and sit on the item. A running cart total shows on both phones; Shane sets a budget per run.
  • Recipes: "Tonight" cooks several dishes to one finish. Longest dish first, it rings when each next dish should go in, re-plans if something starts late, quick access to each dish's steps.
  • Weekly ads: Claude fetches and parses the ads (fetch_weekly_ad), matches them to the list, and pushes per-item verdicts ("Aldi wins", "Publix BOGO", "no deal"). The app shows the verdict on the item and a one-line summary per store.
  • Quantities: items carry a planned quantity ("Pasta, 3 boxes"). A multi-buy coupon that needs more than planned opens a quantity sheet (stepper, live math, "coupon applies" or "1 more unlocks it"); the total uses the real count.
  • Budget: set per run by Shane, seen by both phones. Under budget: quiet. Projected over: amber pill and a strip naming the 2–3 non-deal items to skip to land under; those items get an amber "could skip this trip" tag. Actually over: red readout plus a persistent card listing what could go back (non-deal items, biggest first) with "I put it back" and "Keep" on each; the total updates the moment one is put back.
  • Stores are per phone, never synced: Shane can be at Walmart while Ronnie is at Publix on the same run. Prices, aisles and deal verdicts follow each phone's store.
  • Scan mismatch: exact / near (pick from 2–3 chips, remembered per barcode) / unknown (add it, or pick from the list).
