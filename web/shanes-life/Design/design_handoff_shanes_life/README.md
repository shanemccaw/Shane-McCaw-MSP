## Overview
Shane's Life is a single-user, ADHD-aware personal operating system. It is the cloud-hosted half of ShanesSurvival (the WPF money app in `desktop/ShanesSurvival/` of `shanemccaw/Shane-McCaw-MSP`): one app, one login, one Postgres. A notification tray leads; one capture box (text / voice / photo) is the only input; rooms open from the tray. Claude does all thinking over MCP; the hosted app stores, shows, shares and checks off. It never calls a model.

The full brief is `uploads/shanes-life-design-contract-pack.md` (copied here as `contract.md`). Every decision below traces to it.

## Money math (FinanceHome semantics, unified with ShanesSurvival)
- **The envelope is the account.** A bill's funded amount is the live NFCU balance of its bill account (Plaid). There is no separate "pocket / assigned" number; Budget Day drafts transfers, Plaid confirms them.
- Per bill: `need = max(0, target − balance)`; bills with no target render "no target yet" and are excluded from every sum (never treated as $0).
- **Available to spend** = DirectDeposit + Σ Spend-account balances (FinanceHome's Safe to Spend). Not reduced by still-to-fund; that number is shown beside it.
- **Still to fund** = Σ need over bills due this cycle (not paid, not skipped). **Urgent** = those with need > 0. **Already handled** = due this cycle with need = 0, paid, or skipped. **Protected** = balance ≥ $1 in a bill account whose due date is outside this cycle.
- **Distribute paycheck** (Budget Day): bills due this cycle plus bills due before the next check, ordered by due day; each row gets min(need, remaining DirectDeposit). Per-row copy and Copy-all produce the NFCU transfer list. Mortgage stays out of the auto-split when it can't be covered; its shortfall is named in the note. **Never executes.**
- **What-if**: amount ≤ available → fits, with what's left until payday and still-to-fund unchanged; otherwise state what's on hand and what is free after payday and the split.
- **Transfer simulator**: resolves DirectDeposit / Spend accounts / bills by name, "H1"/"H2" qualifier, or last four; applies the move to copies; reports destination funded/short, new available, new still-to-fund; flags "borrowed from a bill" when the source bill ends up short. **Never executes** (Plaid read-only; NFCU has no Transfer product).
- Capture grammar: "paid X" marks the bill paid this cycle and decrements months-behind and owed by one payment (reaching 0 posts a Win, "X brought current"); "skip X" drops it from this cycle's split; "X is about 40 [on the 20th]" sets a target and due day; "give X 300" / "put 300 toward X" simulates from DirectDeposit; "smoked" / "bought a pack" counts.
- Budget Day = `income_sources.next_pay_date`, every `pay_frequency_days` (14): the cycle rolls (Sep 11 – 24), DirectDeposit gains the check, Spent resets to 0, "due today" notes become "was due the 7th".
- **Forecast** (› on the cycle card): pool = available + expected check; rows = this cycle's unpaid Urgent bills (carried) then bills with `dueDay` inside the next cycle's window (`[11, 24]`; `[25, 8]` wraps the month) that are not paid or skipped; each row takes min(need, pool) in due order; `left = pool − Σ need`. No-target bills are listed, not counted. Read-only; nothing is scheduled.
- Cigarettes card shows only while still-to-fund > available.
