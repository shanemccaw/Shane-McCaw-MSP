Lifecycle

Sprawl isn’t a cleanup project. It’s the absence of a lifecycle.

Delete the dead teams today and you’re back here in eighteen months. The fix is a loop that runs without you — naming, ownership, expiry and guest review enforced from the day a team is born.

Guests are the sharp edge

A guest invited for one project keeps access until someone removes it — and nobody’s job is removing it. Guest review with expiry turns “forever” into “until the review says otherwise”, and the Security Engine flags the stale ones that slip through.

Phones and rooms are policy too

Teams Phone and Teams Rooms fail the same way — licences assigned ad hoc, calling policies copied from whoever set up the last one. Both are named projects below, configured once and then watched for drift like everything else.
