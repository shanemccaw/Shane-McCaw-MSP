When Microsoft changes its mind
Edited after publication — a sharing-label post quietly rewritten six weeks after teams had planned against it. The engine diffs every post nightly and flags the edit.
Date moved twice — EWS throttling slipped from March to June to January. Three remediation windows were held open for nothing.
Withdrawn — the Loop retirement that never happened. The plan built on it was closed the day the post changed.
