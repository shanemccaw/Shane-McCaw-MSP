Remediation, in sequence

Closing access is riskier than finding it. So it happens in waves.

Kill every open link overnight and you break live tenders, auditor access and half of Finance’s month-end. Exposure is retired in a sequence agreed with the business — and the Drift Engine keeps it from quietly coming back.

Why architecture is the other half

Oversharing is usually a symptom. Sites without a hub structure, permissions customised because the structure didn’t fit, metadata nobody maintains — information architecture decides whether people find things or give up. It also decides what Copilot can surface, and to whom.
