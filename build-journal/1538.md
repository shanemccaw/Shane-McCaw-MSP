## Log
- 2026-08-30 ⏳ IN FLIGHT — Read #1538 body/comment and #1539 (foundation) body/comments.
  Building on #1539's fix-route resolver: a new findings-derived checklist wire
  contract (`GET/PUT /api/portal/remediation/checklist`) that joins
  `msp_diagnostic_findings` (latest scan, the tenant's actual open findings) to
  `remediation_knowledge_base` by `checkKey` for fix content, resolves fix route via
  `resolveFixRoute`, and keys the customer's tracker claim (`remediation_tracker_steps`)
  by `checkKey` — the finding's own stable identity — instead of a hand-authored
  step id, so resolving the finding is what resolves the item. Ends at the wire
  contract; no `artifacts/portal` page (no design export for this module).
