## What shipped

A DB-level fix rather than wiring ten call sites, as the issue body proposed. `rbac_sync_user_roles(user_id)` plus AFTER INSERT and AFTER UPDATE OF role, msp_role, can_approve_changes triggers on `users` (the UPDATE trigger only fires when a value actually changes). It covers every present and future writer, including raw SQL, seeds and tests, in the same transaction as the write. Each role converges from its real source of truth today:

- **Rung, both systems:** from role/msp_role, with the role='admin' promotion to PlatformAdmin. Only the seven real rungs count, since msp_role is unchecked text.
- **cap.changes.approve:** follows can_approve_changes both ways. That column is still what the change-control routes read.
- **cap.purchases.approve:** revoked when the rung falls below MSPOperator. It is **not** converged from can_approve_purchases: #2460 moved that grant onto rows, so the column is dead.
- **cap.team.manage:** untouched. Row-native since #2460, and the rule is rung-independent.
- **Existing users:** a backfill ran for all 9 local users; 0 had drifted locally.

No authorization decision moved. rbac-ladder.ts and rbac-capability.ts still take the rung from the JWT claim or the column. Their comments that said nothing writes a rung row are corrected.
