Fix e8a11b6b0: track background tasks from the real stream-json lifecycle (task_started/task_updated/task_notification/background_tasks_changed); a pure `DecideIdleFinalize` defers while they're open, capped by the new `InteractiveBackgroundTaskMaxWaitMinutes` (default 30, 0 = old behavior), with a 60s reaction grace after a task ends. `GetInteractiveState` reports Working (not the amber needs-input cue) during the wait, and Build Watch lists the tasks.
