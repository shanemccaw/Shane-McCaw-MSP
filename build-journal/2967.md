Reason: `pay()` called `onProcessingChange(true)` **before** `await confirmPayment()`, which flipped the parent `payState` to `"processing"` and **unmounted `RetainerPaymentForm` (and its `<div ref={mountRef}>`) mid-confirm** — destroying the Payment Element while Stripe was still confirming it. Every attempt failed this way (matches "every retainer purchase fails"). The originally-requested `pay()` timeout would also have been useless: the form was unmounted and the parent ignored `onProcessingChange(false)`.
