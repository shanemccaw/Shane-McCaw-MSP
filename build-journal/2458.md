  **What shipped.** `artifacts/api-server/src/middlewares/rbac-ladder.ts` resolves the effective
  rung from the verified JWT — the same `role === "admin"` → PlatformAdmin promotion, through the
  cited transcription — and evaluates `ladder.<floor>` against the platform-scoped
  `msp_feature_role_mapping` rows via #2455's shared evaluator. #2457 seeded those allow sets by
  joining the ladder to itself on `idx >= idx`, so the new rule *is* `roleIndex(held) >=
  roleIndex(floor)`, enumerated rather than evaluated. `requireRole`'s signature, its 403 body and
  all **616** real call sites in `src/routes` are untouched.

  **Deliberate scope lines, stated rather than left implicit.** The principal is still identified
  from the JWT: re-resolving roles from `msp_user_roles` per request would change behaviour in
  both directions (a demotion landing mid-session, a promotion landing without re-login) on a step
  whose whole contract is that nothing observable changes — that belongs to #2459/#2460 with the
  claim's retirement. Mapping rows are read `msp_id IS NULL` only, with `orgId: null` passed to the
  evaluator, because an MSP-scoped override of `ladder.platform-admin` would let one MSP grant its
  own staff the top of the platform ladder. And an unseeded or unreadable model answers **503 with
  a loud error log naming #2457's migration, never 403** — Staging genuinely does not have those
  rows yet (they are on #1630), and reporting a missing migration as a permission decision is the
  one thing that would make this cutover undiagnosable. There is no fallback to the old ladder.

  **`ROLE_ORDER` / `roleIndex()` are no longer read anywhere on the request path** — this issue's
  own finish line. The last two readers went with it: `msp-sales-offers.ts`'s SSE route had grown a
  hand-copied `ROLE_ORDER` array (it cannot use `requireRole` — EventSource sets no Authorization
  header), and `msp-settings.ts`'s target-role ceiling check (Git #3032). Both fail-closed edges of
  the ceiling check's old arithmetic are preserved explicitly.
