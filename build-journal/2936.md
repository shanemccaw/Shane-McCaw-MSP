## What shipped

**1. The cascade is one conjunct, not a parallel mechanism.** Shane's instruction was to reuse
#2765's own gate/clock rather than build a second one. The whole cascade is therefore an extra
AND in `decideTenantBillingActive()` and its SQL twin `tenantBillingActiveCondition()`:

    active = tenants.status running
             AND (no subscription rows OR one is active)
             AND the parent MSP's own platform subscription has not lapsed

Everything downstream follows with no further wiring, because it all already reads that one
predicate: `syncTenantRetentionState()` stamps `subscription_lapsed_at` and freezes the
per-record clocks, `post-termination.ts` starts the 7-year window from that instant, and the
gate middleware turns every non-allowlisted route into the wall.

**2. The un-cascade is the same predicate read again.** No resume path was written: the
"JUST RETURNED" branch already resumes clocks from where they froze and clears the lapse
instant, and does not care whether the customer or their MSP came back.

**3. Which MSP states cascade.** `msp_subscriptions.status IN (canceled, unpaid)` OR
`dunning_state IN (access_revoked, archival_flagged)` — the same pair `msp-entitlement.ts`
already uses to close the MSP's OWN access, so a customer's portal can never close before
their MSP's did. `past_due`, `reminder_sent` and `suspended` deliberately do not cascade.
**An MSP with NO `msp_subscriptions` row is not a lapse** — the deploy-safety property; the
opposite reading would gate every customer of every MSP and start seven-year clocks on deploy.
Confirmed against the real local DB: one `msp_subscriptions` row exists (msp_id 1626) while
every tenant sits under msp_id 1.

**4. Immediate, not eventual.** `cascadeMspSubscriptionToCustomers(mspId)` is called from the
four real write sites — `handleMspDunningAdvance`, `handleSubscriptionUpdated`,
`handleSubscriptionDeleted`, `handlePaymentSucceeded`. One function for both directions
(it is a reconciliation, not a transition hook). Never throws; the daily sweep is the backstop.

**5. Limited access, not a hard lockout.** Gate allowlist gained `/portal/deletion-request`
(already existed, just unreachable) and `/portal/retention/reinstatement` (new). Gate body
gained `billingSource: "msp_subscription"`, `mspLapsed`, `mspSubscriptionStatus`,
`mspDunningState` so the wall can say *"your MSP hasn't paid"* without claiming the
customer's own subscription ended.

## Blocker on the environment, worked around, not fixed

The MAIN checkout's per-package `node_modules` trees are absent (`artifacts/api-server` has
only `.vite-temp`, `lib/db` has none), and `link-deps.mjs` junctions those into every
worktree — so neither `tsc` nor `vitest` could run on arrival. Already filed by concurrent
builds as **#2985** and **#2979**; I duplicated it as **#3000** before searching and have
flagged that. No `pnpm install` was run or prescribed. Worked around inside this worktree only
by junctioning each `.pnpm/node_modules` leaf at its realpath and then re-pointing
`@workspace/*` at this worktree's own `lib/*` (the #2121 hazard — realpath sends them into
main, which silently hides in-worktree schema edits from `tsc`). Recipe added to #2985.
