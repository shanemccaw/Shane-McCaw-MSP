## What the placeholder actually was (confirmed, not assumed)

#2765's gate and clock read `tenants.status` through the single constant
`RETENTION_CLOCK_RUNNING_TENANT_STATUSES = ["active","onboarding"]`
(`lib/db/src/schema/retention.ts`), via `isRunningStatus()` in
`artifacts/api-server/src/lib/retention/subscription-state.ts`. That constant was the
one place #2765 isolated so it could be replaced — and it is what was replaced.

## The design question, and how it was settled

Confirmed in code, not assumed: the platform has **two real billing channels**.
`msp-marketplace-purchase.ts` charges the **MSP's** card at the wholesale price
(`catalog-pricing.ts`) and creates the Stripe Subscription on the MSP's Stripe customer
on a named customer's behalf; `tenants.stripe_customer_id` (#490) is the direct channel
where the customer pays. So `billing_party` is a real, observed dimension, not invented.

Two things were settled without inventing anything:

- **Absence of a subscription row is NOT cancellation.** Every existing tenant predates
  the table; treating absence as "not paying" would gate every live customer and start
  their 7-year purge clocks from a migration. `tenants.status` remains the fallback for
  those tenants, and `billingSource` reports which rule decided.
- **`past_due` keeps the portal open.** Real in-repo precedent: `msp-entitlement.ts:79`
  revokes on `dunning_state` reaching `access_revoked`, not on `past_due`.

One genuine product decision was **not** invented and is filed instead — see #2936.
