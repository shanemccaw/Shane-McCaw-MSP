## What the placeholder actually was (confirmed, not assumed)

#2765's gate and clock read `tenants.status` through the single constant
`RETENTION_CLOCK_RUNNING_TENANT_STATUSES = ["active","onboarding"]`
(`lib/db/src/schema/retention.ts`), via `isRunningStatus()` in
`artifacts/api-server/src/lib/retention/subscription-state.ts`. That constant was the one
place #2765 deliberately isolated so it could be replaced — and it is what was replaced.

## The design question, and how it was settled without inventing

Confirmed in code: the platform has **two real billing channels**.
`msp-marketplace-purchase.ts` charges the **MSP's** card at the wholesale price
(`catalog-pricing.ts`) and creates the Stripe Subscription on the MSP's Stripe customer on
a named customer's behalf; `tenants.stripe_customer_id` (#490) is the direct channel where
the customer pays. So `billing_party` is an observed dimension, not an invented one.

Two things settled from real in-repo evidence:

- **Absence of a subscription row is NOT cancellation.** Every existing tenant predates the
  table; treating absence as "not paying" would gate every live customer and start their
  7-year purge clocks from a migration. `tenants.status` remains the fallback for those
  tenants, and the resolved state's `billingSource` reports which rule decided.
- **`past_due` keeps the portal open.** Precedent: `msp-entitlement.ts:79` revokes MSP
  access on `dunning_state` reaching `access_revoked`, not on `past_due`.

One genuine product decision was **not** invented and is filed as **#2936** instead: whether
an MSP's own lapsed platform subscription cascades to its customers' portals and starts
their 7-year purge clocks. No cascade is implemented.

## What shipped

- `tenant_subscriptions` (Drizzle in `lib/db/src/schema/msp.ts` + manual migration, run
  against local Postgres): per-customer subscription state — `billing_party`, Stripe ids,
  status, period, cancel/end instants, `source`. Partial-unique on
  `stripe_subscription_id`.
- `artifacts/api-server/src/lib/tenant-billing-rules.ts` — the pure decision.
- `artifacts/api-server/src/lib/tenant-billing-state.ts` — `resolveTenantBillingState()`,
  the SQL twin `tenantBillingActiveCondition()`, `recordTenantSubscription()`,
  `syncTenantSubscriptionFromStripe()`.
- Rewired to it: the subscription gate, `syncTenantRetentionState()`,
  `findTenantsNeedingRetentionSync()` (the daily sweep's predicate), and
  `postTerminationScheduleFor()` — the last of which would otherwise have suppressed a
  cancelled-but-status-active customer's purge schedule forever.
- Two real write paths: the marketplace purchase now persists the Stripe subscription it
  creates, and `customer.subscription.updated/deleted` now match customer subscriptions
  (previously dropped by the `msp_subscriptions` lookup missing) and reconcile retention.
- Gate/status responses carry `billingSource`, `subscriptionStatus`, `planName`,
  `currentPeriodEnd` — all real or null, never a placeholder.
