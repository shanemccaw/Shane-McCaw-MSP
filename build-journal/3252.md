    `padding: max(0.9rem, env(safe-area-inset-top)) 16px 18px;` — reclaiming the same

    scene breaks out above `.view`'s own padding box. Side padding stays 16px so the
    content still lines up with the rest of the page's margins.
  - `.today-sky`/`.today-night`/`.today-wx-sky` were untouched (`position:absolute;
    inset:0`) — they now fill the real, expanded edge-to-edge box automatically.
