# #1628 — Chat context meter is stateless — accumulate, latch, persist

- **Status:** ⏳ IN FLIGHT
- **Scope:** buildconsole
- **Started:** 2026-08-29

## Log
- 2026-08-29 ⏳ IN FLIGHT — successor to #1468 (virtualization plateau, fixed) on the same
  claude.ai context bar. Second root cause: the whole scrape→UI pipeline is stateless — every
  2s poll recomputes the total from whatever is currently mounted and `UpdateContextMeter`
  overwrites state unconditionally, so DOM churn (a heavy turn unmounting, a mid-render/streaming
  poll) drops the bar even though the real conversation only grew. Fix per #1628 body: an in-page
  accumulator keyed by stable per-message identity (`aria-posinset`, fallback transcript-row
  index) and by conversation id, that never deletes an unmounted row and reports the accumulated
  sum; aria-setsize extrapolation only for turns evicted before ever observed; a per-conversation
  monotonic high-water clamp in-script; host-side persistence of `{conversationId → estTokens,
  turnCount, heavyTurnCount}` (new `ChatContextMeterStore`, mirroring `ChatUrlStore`) that clamps
  incoming values to the persisted high-water and restores a reopened chat; plus removing the
  `_contextMeters` entry when its WebView unloads (the pre-existing leak).
