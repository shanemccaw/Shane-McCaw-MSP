    `information_schema.columns`. Logged to #1630.
  - `POST /portal/risk-register/:rbdId/accept` (`portal-risk-register.ts`):
    resolves the risk's `checkKey` to a workload; when one resolves, only a
    CURRENT Accountable holder on that workload may sign (409 if the workload
    has zero holders, 403 if the caller isn't one of them, with holder names in
    the error detail). A `checkKey` resolving to no workload (free-standing
    liability record, or a cross-cutting check category) keeps the pre-#1511
    behaviour — any `CustomerUser` may sign — the honest unresolved case, not a
    regression. `GET /portal/risk-register` now also serves `authority` (live,
    on every row) and `accepted.authorizedBy` (point-in-time, on accepted rows)
    via the batched resolver.
