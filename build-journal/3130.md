  in every route.

  mechanism, `.empty` padding trimmed 2rem → 1.5rem top/bottom, and the icon bumped
  64px → 88px (candidate #3) for real visual presence now that the surrounding box is
  correctly sized. Verified with real before/after screenshots (headless Edge, real
