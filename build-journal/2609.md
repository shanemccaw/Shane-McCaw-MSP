Invoices cannot be silently mapped onto the selected tenant without inventing a correlation that does not exist in the schema — so the Invoices tab gets its own independent client-user picker (`GET /msp/:mspId/clients`, new) rather than inheriting the page's selected tenant. Documented plainly in the module's own UI copy, not hidden.
  UX decision (build requirement #4): four distinct capabilities → a tabbed `BillingScreen.tsx` wrapper (Subscription | Seat Pricing | Retainer Switch | Invoices) replaces the prior flat vertical stack of just the two modules — a flat stack of four was going to be an unreadably long scroll, and Invoices genuinely needs its own scoped picker UI that doesn't belong bleeding into the tenant-scoped tabs. One agent-built banner at the top of the whole screen (not per-tab), since the entire Billing screen lacks a design export.

Also adding `GET /msp/:mspId/clients` to `msp-invoices.ts` for the invoice client picker (same reasoning).
