Measured before fix: 14 independent 4-way-fork chains (70 nodes) → 57 lanes, forks averaging 28.5 lanes from their own parent, only 1/56 within a single lane of it; even 10 independent single-blocker chains (20 items) → 11 lanes. Fix: precompute (order-only, doesn't touch column assignment) the last row each key is ever claimed as a parent, then free a key's lane the moment (a) a node itself will never have children of its own, or (b) it was the last child claiming its parent's lane and forked elsewhere — and pick the *nearest* free lane to the parent's column for a fork instead of scanning left-to-right. Re-measured after fix: same 70-node scenario → 2 lanes, every fork within 1 lane of its parent; 10-chain scenario → 2 lanes.
