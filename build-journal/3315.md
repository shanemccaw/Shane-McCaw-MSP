## Log
- 2026-09-09T00:00:00Z ⏳ IN FLIGHT — Confirmed root cause in `web/shanes-life/public/app.js`'s
  `openScanSheet()`: `dialog.showModal()` was called only at the very end of the function, after
  the entire async `getUserMedia`/`decodeFromConstraints` camera setup already ran. A native
  `<dialog>` without `showModal()`/`show()` is `display:none` per spec, so the video element was
  still hidden/unpainted while the camera stream started — matches Shane's exact live-device
  symptom (permission granted, no error, but the feed never paints). This also silently broke the
  function's own `if (!dialog.open) { controls.stop(); }` early-close guard, since `dialog.open`
  was always false at that check (showModal hadn't run yet), causing it to stop the scan controls
  immediately after every successful camera start.
