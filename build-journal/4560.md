  **The fix.** New `PillarStatSource` kind `{ kind: "check", checkKey, valueField,
  denominatorField }` resolving a named field off the tenant's real latest observation, removing
  both registry failure modes at once: the `sourceKey` claim that rots silently, and
  `pickMappedValueField`'s token heuristic picking the wrong numeric field of a multi-field check
  under a customer-facing caption (`governance:ownerless-groups` carries both
  `ownerlessGroupCount` = 26 and `_itemCount` = 104 — the heuristic could have shown either).
  `PILLAR_STAT_SPECS` rewritten to the **design's own** per-pillar tiles, which name real current
  keys — so this is the design spec, not my guess, which is what kept it clear of #357's
  deliberate "not a rewrite" decision on `compliance:overshared-sites`.

  observation per check, shared by the tiles and by a new per-pillar coverage roll-up
