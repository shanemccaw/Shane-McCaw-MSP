  **The #1975 conjunction trap caught one, as warned.** `POST /applications/{id}/owners/$ref`
  documents `Application.ReadWrite.OwnedBy **and** Directory.Read.All` — one
  requirement of two permissions, not two options — and the page states outright
  that OwnedBy "isn't sufficient to add another owner. Consent also to
  Application.ReadWrite.All." Reading that cell as alternatives would have shipped
  a rule that 403s at runtime.

  That rule also exposed a real modelling gap, fixed here: `grantRecommended` marks
  a RULE, but this one needs a permission the platform lacks (Directory.Read.All)
  plus one it holds (Application.ReadWrite.All). Both were being reported as "not
  requested", contradicting `DERIVED_WRITE_APP_PERMISSIONS` and — worse —
  suppressing a genuinely missing permission from the admin route's `missing` list,
  which filters on `notRequested`. `DOCUMENTED_BUT_NOT_REQUESTED` and the per-lookup
  `notRequested` now report only permissions the platform actually lacks.
